韩life 隐私政策 · Privacy Policy

生效日期 Effective date：2026 年 11 月 16 日 / November 16, 2026

中文

一、简单说

  • 韩life 不需要注册账号，开发者没有自己的服务器，不会收集、出售或分享你的个人信息。
  • App 里没有广告，也不追踪你在其他 App 或网站上的行为。
  • 你的学习进度、笔记、日记、聊天记录和游戏记录都保存在你自己的设备上。只有你主动使用下面说明的功能时，相关内容才会离开设备。
  • App 内购买（「完整人生」、转盘币礼包）由 Apple 的 App Store 处理，开发者收不到你的付款信息。

二、保存在你设备上的信息

以下内容只保存在你的 iPhone（以及配对的 Apple Watch、桌面小组件）上，开发者看不到：

  • 学习进度：学过的单词、语法、造句、考考自己的结果、「还没记住」的单词等；
  • 你写下或说出的内容：随手记、日记、造句、记忆方法等；
  • 聊天记录：和游戏里的网友、和 Melorin 的消息，你教给 Melorin 的单词、句子和用法（Melorin 的知识本），以及你选的韩语水平；
  • 游戏进度：小人的名字和外形、生日、性格、人生经历、转盘币、收藏品、小电视图鉴等（这些都是游戏里的虚构角色资料，请不要填写真实的个人信息）；
  • 设置：语言、主题、AI 服务商设置等；
  • 你填写的 AI 服务 API Key：保存在设备的钥匙串（Keychain）里，只用于向你选择的 AI 服务商发送请求。

iPhone 和 Apple Watch 之间、App 和桌面小组件之间会在设备本地互相传递学习和游戏数据，用来显示单词、小人和转盘结果。

三、iCloud 备份和文件备份（可选）

  • 你可以在设置里手动把进度备份到 iCloud。备份保存在你自己的 iCloud 账号的私有数据库中，由 Apple 依照其隐私政策保管，开发者无法访问。
  • 你也可以把进度导出成文件，自己保存在「文件」App 或其他位置。

四、AI 功能（可选，需要你自己的 API Key）

AI 批改、问问 AI、随手记讲解、和网友聊天、和 Melorin 聊天、AI 出课、关联词、AI 听写等功能，需要你自己在设置里选择 AI 服务商并填写自己的 API Key。开启后：

  • 你在使用这些功能时输入的文字、你录下的语音（用于听写），以及完成功能所需的内容，会从你的设备直接发送到你选择的服务商，例如 Anthropic（Claude）、OpenAI（ChatGPT）、Google（Gemini）、DeepSeek、阿里云（通义千问）、月之暗面（Kimi）、智谱、火山方舟（豆包）、OpenRouter，或你自己填写的其他兼容接口；
  • 「完成功能所需的内容」包括：学过的单词；和网友聊天时，你们之前的聊天记录、你选的韩语水平和小人的名字；和 Melorin 聊天时，你们之前的聊天记录和 Melorin 的知识本；
  • 这些内容不会经过开发者，开发者也看不到；
  • 服务商如何处理这些内容，以该服务商的隐私政策和服务条款为准，费用也由你和服务商结算；
  • 不填写 API Key，就不会有任何内容发送给 AI 服务商。

五、麦克风和语音识别

  • 只有在你点「说话」「说出来」等按钮时，App 才会使用麦克风。
  • 语音转文字可能使用 Apple 的语音识别服务，按 Apple 的隐私政策处理；如果你开启了 AI 听写，录音会按第四条发送给你选择的 AI 服务商。
  • 录音只用于这一次转文字，App 不会长期保存录音。

六、通知

  • 游戏里的网友过几分钟才会回复。如果你允许通知，App 会在你的设备上预约一条本地通知，到时间提醒你「网友回复你了」。
  • 这些通知由你的设备自己发出，不经过开发者或任何推送服务器。你可以随时在 iPhone「设置 › 通知」中关闭。

七、手写识别（Google ML Kit）

  • 手写功能使用 Google 的 ML Kit 手写识别。第一次使用时会从 Google 下载语言模型，之后在设备上识别你的笔迹。
  • 按照 Google 公布的说明，ML Kit 会向 Google 发送用于诊断和使用统计的信息：设备信息（如机型、系统版本）、App 信息（如 Bundle ID、版本）、不用于识别个人的安装标识、性能数据、API 设置、事件类型、错误代码，以及设置的识别语言。Google 公布的数据列表中不包括你的笔迹内容。详见 Google 的说明：https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/ios-data-disclosure

八、购买

  • 购买「完整人生」和转盘币礼包由 Apple 通过 App Store 处理，开发者收不到你的付款信息（例如银行卡号）。
  • App 只在你的设备上读取 Apple 提供的购买记录，用来确认是否已经解锁、转盘币有没有到账；为了不重复发转盘币，礼包的交易编号会记在你设备上的存档里。这些都不会上传到任何地方（开发者没有服务器）。

九、儿童

韩life 不是专门面向 13 岁以下儿童设计的。游戏中包含恋爱、结婚、虚拟彩票、虚拟股票等情节，未成年人请在家长陪同下使用。

十、删除你的数据

  • 删除 App，就会删除保存在设备上的所有数据（包括钥匙串里的 API Key）。
  • iCloud 备份可以在 iPhone「设置 › [你的名字] › iCloud › 管理储存空间」中删除。
  • 发送给 AI 服务商的内容，请按该服务商提供的方式管理或删除。

十一、政策更新

如果以后功能有变化（例如增加由开发者提供的 AI 服务、订阅或账号功能），我们会更新这份政策，并在 App 或 App Store 页面中说明。

English

1. In short

  • HanLife does not require an account. The developer runs no servers and does not collect, sell, or share your personal information.
  • There are no ads and no tracking across other apps or websites.
  • Your progress, notes, diary entries, chats, and game records stay on your device. Data leaves your device only when you use one of the optional features described below.
  • In-app purchases (Full Life and Spin Coin packs) are handled by Apple through the App Store. The developer never receives your payment information.

2. Information stored on your device

The following is stored only on your iPhone (and your paired Apple Watch and Home Screen widgets). The developer cannot see it:

  • Learning progress: words and grammar you've studied, your sentences, quiz results, words you're still reviewing, and similar data.
  • Content you write or say: quick notes, diary entries, sentences, and memory tips.
  • Chats: your messages with in-game online friends and with Melorin, the words, sentences, and usage notes you teach Melorin (Melorin's notes), and the Korean level you choose.
  • Game progress: your character's name, look, birthday, personality, life events, Spin Coins, collectibles, TV Collection, and similar data. These are fictional character details. Please don't enter real personal information.
  • Settings: language, theme, and AI provider settings.
  • Your AI API key, stored in the device Keychain and used only to send requests to the AI provider you choose.

Learning and game data is passed locally between your iPhone and Apple Watch, and between the app and its widgets, to show words, your character, and spin results.

3. iCloud and file backups (optional)

  • You can back up your progress to iCloud from Settings. The backup is stored in your own iCloud account's private database and is handled by Apple under its privacy policy. The developer cannot access it.
  • You can also export your progress as a file and keep it in the Files app or anywhere you like.

4. AI features (optional, your own API key)

Features such as AI feedback, Ask AI, Quick Note explanations, chatting with online friends, chatting with Melorin, AI lessons, related words, and AI transcription require you to choose an AI provider and enter your own API key in Settings. When you use them:

  • The text you enter, voice recordings you make for transcription, and the content needed for the feature are sent directly from your device to the provider you chose, for example Anthropic (Claude), OpenAI (ChatGPT), Google (Gemini), DeepSeek, Alibaba Cloud (Qwen), Moonshot AI (Kimi), Zhipu, Volcano Ark (Doubao), OpenRouter, or another compatible endpoint you enter.
  • “Content needed for the feature” includes words you've studied; for online friends, your earlier messages with them, the Korean level you chose, and your character's name; and for Melorin, your earlier messages with her and Melorin's notes.
  • This content does not pass through the developer, and the developer cannot see it.
  • The provider handles it under its own privacy policy and terms. Any usage fees are between you and the provider.
  • If you don't enter an API key, nothing is sent to any AI provider.

5. Microphone and speech recognition

  • The app uses the microphone only when you tap a voice button such as “Say it.”
  • Speech-to-text may use Apple's speech recognition service, which is handled under Apple's privacy policy. If you turn on AI transcription, your recording is sent to your chosen AI provider as described in Section 4.
  • Recordings are used only for that transcription. The app doesn't keep them.

6. Notifications

  • In-game online friends reply after a few minutes. If you allow notifications, the app schedules a local notification on your device to let you know when a friend has replied.
  • These notifications come from your device itself, not from the developer or any push server. You can turn them off anytime in Settings › Notifications on your iPhone.

7. Handwriting recognition (Google ML Kit)

  • Handwriting uses Google ML Kit Digital Ink Recognition. The first time you use it, a language model is downloaded from Google. After that, your handwriting is recognized on your device.
  • According to Google's published disclosure, ML Kit sends diagnostics and usage analytics to Google: device information (such as model and OS version), app information (such as bundle ID and version), a per-installation identifier that is not intended to identify you, performance metrics, API configuration, event types, error codes, and the configured recognition languages. Google's published list doesn't include your handwriting. See https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/ios-data-disclosure

8. Purchases

  • Purchases of Full Life and Spin Coin packs are processed by Apple through the App Store. The developer doesn't receive your payment information, such as card numbers.
  • The app reads Apple's purchase records on your device only to check whether Full Life is unlocked and whether Spin Coins have been delivered. To avoid delivering coins twice, pack transaction IDs are kept in the save on your device. None of this is uploaded anywhere, because the developer has no servers.

9. Children

HanLife is not directed at children under 13. The game includes dating, marriage, and in-game lottery and stock features. Minors should use it with a parent or guardian.

10. Deleting your data

  • Deleting the app deletes all data stored on your device, including the API key in the Keychain.
  • To delete iCloud backups, go to Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Storage on your iPhone.
  • For content sent to an AI provider, use that provider's tools to manage or delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

If features change (for example, if we add developer-provided AI, subscriptions, or accounts), we'll update this policy and let you know in the app or on the App Store page.
