宝贝手账 隐私政策 · Privacy Policy

生效日期 Effective date：2026 年 11 月 16 日 / November 16, 2026

中文

一、简单说

  • 宝贝手账（英文名 Pets' Log）不需要注册账号，不联网，开发者没有自己的服务器。
  • 开发者不会收集、看到、出售或分享你的任何信息。
  • App 里没有广告，没有第三方统计或追踪工具，也不追踪你在其他 App 或网站上的行为。
  • 你记下的所有内容都只保存在你自己的设备上。

二、保存在你设备上的信息

以下内容只保存在你的 iPhone（以及同一台手机上的桌面小组件）里，开发者看不到：

  • 宝贝的档案：名字、种类、性别、生日、体重、照片、性格、家族关系等；
  • 病例和病程：症状、就诊、检查、手术、医嘱、药敏结果、你选择保存的照片（如检查报告照片）；
  • 用药和打卡记录、复诊和拆线日期；
  • 药品库：药名、库存、有效期、用途和剂量备注；
  • 设置：主题、字体、语言等。

App 和桌面小组件通过同一台设备上的共享存储读写这些数据，用来在桌面上显示今天的用药并直接打卡。

三、照片

  • 你给宝贝设置头像、给病程添加照片时，App 会打开系统的照片选择器。只有你选中的那张照片会被复制进 App，App 不会读取你的其他照片。
  • 照片和其他记录一样，只保存在你的设备上。

四、通知

  • 用药、复诊、拆线、药品快用完或快过期的提醒，都是在你的设备上安排的本地通知，不经过任何服务器。
  • 你第一次添加需要提醒的内容时，系统会询问是否允许通知。你可以随时在 iPhone「设置 › 通知 › 宝贝手账」里更改。

五、备份和恢复

  • 你可以在「爱宠」页左上角手动导出备份文件，自己选择保存到「文件」App、AirDrop 或其他位置。备份文件包含你的全部记录和照片，请妥善保管。
  • 如果你开启了 iPhone 的 iCloud 备份，App 的数据可能会随整机备份一起由 Apple 保管，按 Apple 的隐私政策处理，开发者无法访问。

六、网页链接

App 的「关于」页面里有隐私政策、用户协议的链接和联系邮箱。点开后会跳到浏览器或邮件 App，由对应的服务处理。

七、儿童

宝贝手账适合所有年龄使用，但并不是专门为 13 岁以下儿童设计的。App 不收集任何人的个人信息，包括儿童。

八、删除你的数据

  • 你可以在 App 里删除任何一只宝贝、病例、记录或药品；
  • 删除 App 会同时删除设备上的全部数据（你自己导出的备份文件除外）。
  • 因为开发者没有保存你的任何数据，所以不需要也无法向开发者申请删除。

九、政策更新

如果以后 App 增加了需要联网或处理数据的新功能，我们会先更新本政策，并在新版本说明里告诉你。更新后的政策发布在本页面。

English

1. In short

  • Pets' Log (Chinese name 宝贝手账) needs no account, does not connect to the internet, and the developer runs no servers.
  • The developer does not collect, see, sell or share any of your information.
  • There are no ads, no third-party analytics or tracking tools, and the app does not track you across other apps or websites.
  • Everything you record stays on your own device.

2. Information stored on your device

The following stays only on your iPhone (and the Home Screen widget on the same phone). The developer cannot see it:

  • Pet profiles: name, species, sex, birthday, weight, photo, personality, family links, etc.;
  • Cases and timelines: symptoms, vet visits, tests, surgery, vet's instructions, sensitivity results, and photos you choose to save (such as lab report photos);
  • Medication schedules, check-offs, and dates for check-ups and stitch removal;
  • Medicine library: names, stock, expiry dates, and notes on use and dose;
  • Settings: theme, font, language, etc.

The app and its widget read and write this data through shared storage on the same device, so you can see and check off today's doses from your Home Screen.

3. Photos

  • When you set a pet's photo or add a photo to a case, the app opens the system photo picker. Only the photo you pick is copied into the app; the app cannot read your other photos.
  • Photos stay on your device like all other records.

4. Notifications

  • Reminders for doses, check-ups, stitch removal, and low or expiring medicines are local notifications scheduled on your device, with no server involved.
  • The system asks for permission the first time you add something that needs a reminder. You can change this at any time in iPhone Settings › Notifications › Pets' Log.

5. Backup and restore

  • You can export a backup file from the top left of the "Pets" tab and save it wherever you like (Files, AirDrop, etc.). The file contains all your records and photos, so please keep it safe.
  • If iCloud Backup is turned on for your iPhone, the app's data may be included in your device backup, which Apple handles under its own privacy policy. The developer cannot access it.

6. Web links

The app's About page links to this policy, the Terms of Use, and a contact email. Opening them takes you to your browser or mail app, which handle them under their own terms.

7. Children

Pets' Log is suitable for all ages but is not specifically designed for children under 13. The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

8. Deleting your data

  • You can delete any pet, case, record or medicine in the app;
  • Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device (except backup files you exported yourself).
  • Because the developer keeps none of your data, there is nothing to request deletion of.

9. Changes to this policy

If a future version adds features that go online or handle data differently, we will update this policy first and mention it in the release notes. The current version is always on this page.
