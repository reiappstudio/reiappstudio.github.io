宝贝手账｜技术支持 · Support

宝贝手账（Pets' Log）是给宠物主人的看病和喂药记录本：宝贝档案、宠物家谱、病例和病程、用药打卡、药品库存提醒、复诊提醒，还有桌面小组件。免费、没有广告、没有内购，不用注册，所有记录只保存在你的手机里。

中文

常见问题

1. 我的记录保存在哪里？会上传吗？

只保存在你自己的 iPhone 上。宝贝手账不联网、没有服务器，开发者看不到你的任何记录。

2. 换手机或删掉 App，记录会丢吗？

删掉 App 会把手机上的记录一起删掉，所以请定期备份：

  • 在「爱宠」页点左上角的云朵小按钮 › 导出备份，把文件存到「文件」App 或其他地方；
  • 在新手机上装好宝贝手账，同一个位置点「从备份文件恢复」，选这个文件就行。备份里包含照片。

3. 为什么收不到喂药提醒？

  • 打开 iPhone「设置 › 通知 › 宝贝手账」，确认「允许通知」是打开的；
  • 检查是不是开着「专注模式」或「勿扰模式」；
  • 提醒只会在你添加了用药、复诊或拆线日期以后才有。

4. 怎么把小组件放到桌面上？

在手机桌面空白处长按 › 点左上角「编辑」或「＋」› 搜索「宝贝手账」› 选「今日喂药」，有小、中、大三种尺寸。在小组件上可以直接点掉今天的用药。

5. 只能给仓鼠、花枝鼠这类小宠物用吗？

不是，猫、狗、兔子、豚鼠、龙猫、蜜袋鼯、鸟、爬宠等都可以，所有宝贝都能用。

6. 宠物家谱怎么用？

在宝贝的档案编辑页里选好它的爸爸妈妈，然后在「爱宠」页点「家族树」，就能看到画出来的家谱和每只宝贝来到家里的时间线。

7. App 能帮我诊断或者算用药剂量吗？

不能。宝贝手账只帮你记录和提醒，不会诊断、推荐药物或计算剂量。用药种类和剂量请一定以兽医医嘱为准；宝贝情况不好时请尽快就医。

8. 怎么换主题、字体或语言？

在「今日」页点左上角的调色盘按钮，可以换 6 种主题、字体和中文 / English。换语言后重新打开 App 生效。

9. 怎么删掉示例数据？

示例宝贝和示例药品的名字后面都带着「（示例）」。打开它的编辑页，滑到最下面点「删除」即可。

10. 要收费吗？

不收费，没有广告，也没有内购。

English

Pets' Log is a care journal for pet owners: pet profiles, a pet family tree, cases and timelines, medication check-offs, medicine stock reminders, check-up reminders and a Home Screen widget. It's free, with no ads and no in-app purchases. No account is needed, and all records stay on your phone.

Questions or ideas? Email us at reiappstudio@gmail.com (we usually reply within a few days). Including your iPhone model, iOS version and a screenshot helps us find the problem faster.

FAQ

1. Where are my records stored? Are they uploaded?

Only on your own iPhone. Pets' Log doesn't go online and has no servers. The developer cannot see any of your records.

2. Will I lose my records if I change phones or delete the app?

Deleting the app also deletes its records from your phone, so please back up regularly:

  • On the "Pets" tab, tap the little cloud button at the top left › Export backup, and save the file to Files or anywhere you like;
  • On your new phone, install Pets' Log, tap the same button › Restore from backup file, and choose that file. Photos are included.

3. Why am I not getting medication reminders?

  • Open iPhone Settings › Notifications › Pets' Log and make sure Allow Notifications is on;
  • Check whether a Focus or Do Not Disturb mode is on;
  • Reminders only appear after you add medication, check-up or stitch-removal dates.

4. How do I add the widget to my Home Screen?

Touch and hold an empty spot on your Home Screen › tap Edit or "+" › search for "Pets' Log" › choose "Today's Meds". It comes in small, medium and large sizes, and you can check off today's doses right on the widget.

5. Is it only for small pets like hamsters and rats?

No. Cats, dogs, rabbits, guinea pigs, chinchillas, sugar gliders, birds, reptiles and more are all welcome.

6. How does the pet family tree work?

In a pet's profile editor, choose its mom and dad. Then tap "Family tree" on the "Pets" tab to see the tree and a timeline of when each pet joined the family.

7. Can the app diagnose my pet or calculate doses?

No. Pets' Log only helps you keep records and reminders. It does not diagnose, recommend medicines or calculate doses. Always follow your vet's instructions, and see a vet promptly if your pet seems unwell.

8. How do I change the theme, font or language?

On the "Today" tab, tap the palette button at the top left to choose from 6 themes, fonts, and Chinese / English. A language change takes effect after you reopen the app.

9. How do I delete the sample data?

Sample pets and medicines have "(sample)" after their names. Open the item's edit page, scroll to the bottom and tap Delete.

10. Does it cost anything?

No. It's free, with no ads and no in-app purchases.
